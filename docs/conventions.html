Pricing inputs

ThingThis repoUsual vendor / desk trap
F put-call parity on the chain at min |C−P|: F = K + erT(C − P) when the Treasury curve is present (method=parity); undiscounted F = K + C − P otherwise (parity-r0). For VIX this is the VX future of that expiry, not a spot VIX. I:SPX print (not entitled)
T ACT/365 to settlement instant (SPY/SPXW 16:00 ET PM; SPX monthlies and VIX/VIXW 09:30 ET AM). On the day-bar path the day count is a passed object (pricing.daycount) rather than a module constant, still defaulting to ACT/365. Two quantities are kept apart there: vol time, the T in σ√T, is what the convention decides; money time, the T in e−rT and the Treasury-curve tenor, is always ACT/365 because interest accrues on weekends. expiry date at UTC midnight understates T (~108 bp IV at 7 DTE); converting the discount factor and the curve tenor along with σ√T
r Treasury curve via rate_for (scalar --r / DRIFT_CHECK_R override) one flat scalar rate for every maturity
