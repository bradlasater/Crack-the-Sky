pricing

← overview

Calculators. Not a surface, not an interpolator. Pass S, K, T, r, q (or F) and sigma explicitly.

GreeksConventions
Defaults: vega per 1.00 vol, theta per year of calendar time, spot delta/gamma. Dual (∂/∂K) is always also populated. Tests that read a Greek must name the convention.
Named catalog
price; 1st delta, dual_delta, vega, theta, rho, rho_dividend; 2nd gamma, dual_gamma, vanna, volga, charm, veta, vera; 3rd speed, zomma, color, ultima; elasticity.
European vs American
Engine protocol: EuropeanBSM closed-form; AmericanCRR tree with bump-and-revalue Greeks. Style comes from the contract (SPY American, SPX/SPXW European).
IV invert
Newton with bounds, Brent fallback. Price outside discounted no-arbitrage bounds → ValueError. Never returns NaN. European BSM only — there is no American IV solver.
