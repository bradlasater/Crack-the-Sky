Formula stubs

← overview

Future anchors. Derivations live in pricing/, not here.

BSM price / d1 / d2 — pricing.bsm. Continuous r, q; or F via F = S e^{(r−q)T}.
IV invert — pricing.iv.implied_vol. Newton, then Brent. Bounds: discounted intrinsic. Never NaN.
CRR tree — pricing.engine.crr_price. American early exercise; risk-neutral p outside [0,1] is an error, not clipped.
Parity forward — ingest.jobs.forward_from_parity. Undiscounted approximation F = K + C − P at the strike minimising |C−P|. Exact parity is F = K + erT(C − P); the job uses the approximation.
Year fraction — pricing.from_market.year_fraction. ACT/365 to settlement instant.
Vendor unit align — theta × 365 (day → year), vega × 100 (1% → 1.00). Same in from_market diffs and drift_check.
docs/formula-stubs.html — Crack the Sky handbook. Index: index.html. Do not commit .env.
