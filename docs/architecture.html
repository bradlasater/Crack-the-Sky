This repo pulls Massive.com (ex-Polygon) Options Developer data for SPY and SPX, lands raw payloads plus clean parquet, and once a day lets the S3 flat file overwrite the live minute-bar capture. Everything live is 15-minute delayed. Cron is the scheduler; jobs are python -m ingest.jobs.<name>.

Architecture

Three pipes into one disk tree. The websocket is the intraday tape (explicit contract subscriptions in chunks of 3,000 — wildcards ACK and deliver nothing on this tier). REST fills reference, snapshots, trades, and end-of-day bars. Flat files on files.massive.com land ~11:00 ET the next day and always win at reconcile.

Market data + Greeks (sibling packages)

Ingest capture behaviour is unchanged, but the shared job runner is not: cli.run_job and ws_minute_bars now send a terminal Healthchecks ping on every exit path (including the market gate's clean exit on holidays, and KeyboardInterrupt), so a skipped or interrupted run reports its outcome instead of sitting in started until it is reported as a hung run. Nothing about what is captured, or where it lands, changed. Two sibling packages sit at repo root: marketdata/ (typed parquet I/O) and pricing/ (calculators, not a surface). Vendor snapshot greeks_* and implied_volatility are diagnostics only — every Greek we use is computed here.

marketdata
OPRA parse of O:{root}{YYMMDD}{C|P}{strike} with allowlist SPY, SPX, SPXW (rejects SPYL/SPXS/SPYG). Contract carries exercise style (American SPY, European SPX/SPXW) and multiplier 100. Catalog lists clean/{dataset}/dt=* for every SCHEMAS key; extra or missing parquet columns fail. As-of reads take the last snapshot file at or before asof_ns (never the whole partition). python -m marketdata.validate exits nonzero on foreign roots, mixed underlyings in a SPY-only query, required-nulls, or an empty allowlisted root.
pricing
Black–Scholes–Merton with continuous r, q (or a forward F = S e^{(r-q)T}). GreeksConventions defaults: vega per 1.00 vol, theta per year of calendar time, spot delta/gamma (dual is also populated). Catalog: price; 1st delta, dual_delta, vega, theta, rho, rho_dividend; 2nd gamma, dual_gamma, vanna, volga/vomma, charm, veta, vera; 3rd speed, zomma, color, ultima; elasticity (lambda). IV invert is Newton + Brent, ValueError never NaN. Engine protocol: EuropeanBSM analytic, AmericanCRR bump-and-revalue. No surface, no interpolator.
Greeks as-of
python -m pricing.from_market --date YYYY-MM-DD --asof-ns N --r 0.04 loads the last option_snapshots and forwards files at or before that instant, inverts own IV, then computes own Greeks. Market price is last_trade_price if present, else day_close — this tier has no NBBO, so there is no mid. Engine: European BSM for SPX/SPXW, American CRR for SPY (default 51 tree steps on the chain; --spy-atm-pct CRR-prices only ATM SPY and European-prices the rest). Vendor greeks_* / implied_volatility are copied as diagnostics with signed diffs (own − vendor); they are never inputs. T is ACT/365 to the settlement instant. --r is a scalar (no rates warehouse). Exits nonzero on foreign roots, mixed underlyings, required-nulls, empty allowlisted root, extra/missing parquet columns, or a price outside no-arbitrage bounds.
Daily drift check
python -m pricing.drift_check at 17:00 ET Mon–Fri, after the 16:35 EOD snapshot. Loads the last snapshots/forwards at or before 16:40 ET, inverts own IV, computes own Greeks, then compares to vendor after unit alignment: vendor theta (per calendar day) × 365 to per-year; vendor vega (per 1% vol) × 100 to per 1.00 vol. IV/delta/gamma need no conversion. Vendor is a canary, not gospel. Compare set is ATM (|K/F − 1| ≤ 5%) with vendor IV present. FAIL when median |ΔIV| > 0.04, or >25% of ATM names exceed the abs/rel band on IV, delta, vega, gamma, or theta — not a single far-OTM tick. Uninvertible / T≤0 / missing quote are skipped and counted. SPY CRR is an ATM slice (--spy-atm-pct 0.05, --max-rows 400) so cron finishes. DRIFT_CHECK_R=0.04 in .env (no rates warehouse). Alert: structured JSONL + _meta/drift_check.json, exit 1 (cron MAILTO if set; Healthchecks massive-drift-check /fail; optional DRIFT_ALERT_WEBHOOK).
Source of schedule: deploy/crontab. Jobs: ingest/jobs/*.py. Do not commit .env.
